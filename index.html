AAAI-27 Bridge · In person

Toward General Autoformalization (TGA)

16–17 February 2027 · Montréal, Canada

Connecting formalized mathematics, verification, logic and declarative programming, automated planning, and knowledge representation around the challenge of translating informal statements into faithful formal representations.

OpenReview submission portal

Submissions open 10 November 2026. Deadline: 1 December 2026, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth.

About the Bridge

Autoformalization plays a growing role in connecting language models with formal tools. TGA brings together communities tackling related challenges in semantic faithfulness, evaluation, robustness, and human involvement. The aim is to exchange methods and build shared vocabulary and collaborations.

Topics

We welcome work across proof assistants (e.g., Lean, Coq, Isabelle), formal logics and verification, Prolog/ASP/Datalog, automated planning (PDDL), and knowledge representation (OWL/RDF), including LLM-based and neuro-symbolic approaches, benchmarks, interfaces, and applications.

Bridge format

Day 1: Education and shared perspectives, with tutorials and hands-on discussion.

Day 2: Research exchange and collaboration, with posters, demos, selected lightning talks, and cross-community discussion.

A detailed programme and speakers: TBA. All presentations are in person.

Call for contributions

  • Research contributions: 2–4 pages, including work in progress or previously published work.
  • Position statements, benchmark proposals, and system/demo descriptions: 1–2 pages.

Page limits exclude references. Submit one PDF through OpenReview. Accepted contributions are expected to be presented as posters or demos; some may be selected for lightning talks.

Publication: TGA is non-archival and does not publish AAAI proceedings. Authors of selected contributions may be invited to share a version on arXiv (or link an existing one), subject to their agreement, publication rights, and arXiv policies.
